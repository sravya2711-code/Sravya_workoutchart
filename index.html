  • Warm-up (3 min): 30 sec each of marching, arm circles, hip circles, bodyweight squats, walking lunges, cat-cow.
  • Weight: use one pair of dumbbells for the whole circuit. Start with 3–4 kg each. The last 2–3 reps of each move should feel hard.
  • Getting stronger: when all 3 rounds feel easy, go up 1 kg. Adding weight every week or two is what builds strength.
  • Alternate-side moves: 12 reps total, switching sides each rep. "Each leg" moves: do all reps on one side, then the other.
  • Form first: if your form slips, slow down or drop the weight.
  • Stretch (2 min): hamstrings, quads, chest and shoulders, 30 sec each.
